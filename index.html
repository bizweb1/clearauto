      Questions
      
Before you book

    

    

      
Do I need to be home?
Not for an oil change, as long as we can reach the car and you leave the keys or hood release accessible. For tint we'll need the car unlocked. We'll text when we arrive and when we're done.

      
How long does it take?
An oil change takes about 30 minutes. Tint depends on the job: front two windows usually take about an hour, a full car a few hours.

      
Where can you work on my car?
A driveway, a parking lot, or a garage with room around the car. Tint needs shade or a garage, so let us know your setup in the notes.

      
What happens to the old oil?
We bring a drip mat, catch everything, and take the used oil and filter with us for recycling. Nothing is left behind.

      
Will this affect my warranty?
No. Using the oil grade your manufacturer specifies keeps your warranty intact, and we note the date and mileage for your records.

      
How do I pay?
You get the exact total before we start and pay once the job is done.
